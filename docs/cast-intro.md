# Cast introduction and local menu theme

The current experience adds a 26-second opening to the unchanged 474.92-second story, for 500.92 seconds total. Eight 2.5-second cards introduce Kernel, Patch, Firewall, Mira, Elara, Axiom, Vex and the Narrator, followed by a six-second title. Role text appears under every name; the Narrator has typography instead of a portrait. Portrait devices show an additional readable name/role below the artwork. Reduced motion uses stable artwork while retaining the authored timing.

The supplied `gta_san_andreas.mp3` is copied byte-for-byte to ignored `.local-media/music/intro-theme.mp3`. SHA-256: `c97f0f8a344d619d383e83f8a126c8a2ef6524a0e8f0cd3dfaaae3f159b64a87`. Original attachment preserved; verified 30.04 seconds, 44.1 kHz stereo, FFmpeg decode successful. The development-only `/local-music/intro-theme.mp3` route allows exactly this filename. Both `.local-media` and `.codex-remote-attachments` are ignored. Public builds include neither recordings nor attachments.

Menu music is enabled by default and attempts autoplay; a click or keypress resumes AudioContext when the browser blocks autoplay and starts the menu loop with a 120 ms boundary crossfade. Menu Settings and Credits retain music. Start Story loads the cached story audio and theme, then fades out menu playback while restarting the theme at the beginning for the cast intro. The final second fades the theme to silence; at experience time 26 the existing story sound adapter receives story time zero. Music volume and master mute control both theme and score. The supplied theme is never added to the story soundtrack.

The single StoryEngine clock covers intro and story. Intro camera motion and source offsets derive from that clock, with speed/pause/seek supported. Skip Intro seeks to 26. Story replay and restart seek to 26; restart while in the intro seeks to zero. Returning to the menu restores its loop; Start Story plays the intro again. Hiding the page stops narrative and menu audio; playback requires deliberate resumption. Missing theme audio leaves the intro and story usable, with a menu notice when music is requested.

`ExperienceAudio` shares the existing mixer/context and delegates narrative time minus 26 to the existing story bus. The cast stage does not alter dialogue, scene direction, fictional countdown or artwork. Existing browser seek helpers and local review scripts now translate story time to experience time.
